📱 ESS — Delete My Account

ESS (Employee Self-Service) is the internal mobile app used by Artistic Garment Industries (AGI) employees to access attendance, salary, and leave information. If you would like your ESS app account and associated login data removed, please use the form below.

Deleting your account removes your ESS login/registration record and any saved credentials on your device. It does not affect your official HR employment record with AGI, which is retained under standard company policy.

You can also email us directly at ammar.btk2@agi.artisticgarment.com from your registered contact, including your CNIC and the subject line "ESS Account Deletion Request." Requests are typically processed within 7 business days.
